How to approve mileage claims

Volunteers submit travel miles. Staff approve or reject. Reimbursement rates stay offline with the charity.

  1. Open Mileage

    Pending claims show first with volunteer, date, miles, and purpose.

  2. Check the trip makes sense

    Match it against the shift or transport run if you can. Miles only are stored here.

  3. Approve or reject

    Approved claims stay on the record for the volunteer. Rejected claims need a clear outcome so they know to resubmit or drop it.

  4. Pay outside ShelterLink

    If the charity reimburses travel, apply your own rate in payroll or expenses. ShelterLink does not pay out.
