How to print a QR check-in poster

Put a poster where people arrive so they check in on their own phone. The tablet kiosk still works.

  1. Find the shift on Shifts

    Open the opportunity you need a poster for.

  2. Open the check-in poster

    Use the Check-in poster link on that shift. It opens a page with the QR code and a backup code.

  3. Print it

    Tap Print poster and put the sheet at reception or the yard gate.

  4. Tell volunteers what to do

    They scan with their phone camera, sign in if asked, then tap Check in. Scan again before leaving and tap Check out.

  5. No camera?

    The poster also shows a short code they can enter from My applications.
