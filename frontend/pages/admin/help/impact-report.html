How to pull an impact report

Totals for a period you choose, for a grant bid or trustee paper. No names or contact details.

  1. Open Impact report

    It is under Insight in the admin menu.

  2. Pick the dates

    From and To cover the grant period or the year you need.

  3. Show figures

    You get hours, volunteers, walks, and animals helped from what is already in ShelterLink.

  4. Download PDF or CSV

    Use PDF for a paper pack. Use CSV if you are pasting into a spreadsheet.

  5. What counts

    Hours are approved hour records in the period. Animal care comes from activity volunteers logged. Anything not recorded here is not in the report.
