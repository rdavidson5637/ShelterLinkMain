How to approve bookings

Clear the applications queue so people know if they are on the rota.

  1. Open Applications

    Pending is the default filter. Waitlisted shows people next in line when a place opens.

  2. Check the person fits the shift

    Look at qualifications, AccessNI, and any notes before you decide.

  3. Approve or reject

    Use the actions on the row. Approved people appear on the day sheet. Rejected people get the outcome on their account.

  4. Booking mode matters

    If the shelter uses staff approval for every booking, every application lands here. If trusted volunteers book instantly, only non-trusted people wait on this page.

  5. Waitlist

    When someone drops out and the shelter uses instant booking for trusted people, a trusted waitlisted volunteer can confirm automatically. Everyone else still needs a decision here.
