How to mark trusted volunteers

Trusted people book into open places without waiting for staff, when the shelter uses that mode.

  1. Only admins can change this

    Staff can see the list. Admins set who is trusted and which booking mode the shelter uses.

  2. Set booking mode first

    On Applications, choose Trusted volunteers book instantly, then Save booking mode. Until that is on, trust has no effect on booking.

  3. Trust someone from an application

    Open the actions menu on one of their applications and choose Trust to book instantly.

  4. Review the trusted list

    The Trusted volunteers details on Applications shows who is on the list. Remove someone there if they should wait for approval again.

  5. Who to trust

    People you already know turn up, finish checks, and do not need a staff eye on every booking. Keep the list short.
