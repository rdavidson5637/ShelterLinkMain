Morning ops guide

A short checklist for opening the volunteer day at this shelter.

  1. Day sheet. Open today’s roster, note gaps, and print or share with reception.

    Open day sheet · Open week sheet

  2. Check-ins. Confirm expected arrivals and kiosk / code check-in for first shifts.

    Use the day sheet check-in status, or the tablet kiosk at reception.

  3. Urgent cover. If a shift is understaffed, flag urgent cover from Opportunities so matching volunteers get email (and push when enabled).

    Manage opportunities

  4. Animals. Review residents on today’s shifts and any handling notes before volunteers arrive.

    Animals

  5. Fosters. Check open foster requests and active placements needing follow-up.

    Fosters

  6. Messages. Scan unread threads for foster or shift questions (replaces WhatsApp for ops).

    Message threads

  7. Applications & hours. Clear pending applications; approve yesterday’s hours so badges and certificates stay accurate.

    Applications · Approve hours

  8. Checks expiring soon. Clear background checks ending in the next 60 days are on the staff home. The list shows name, type, status, and date.

    Staff home

If something goes wrong

  • Volunteer cannot apply: check profile approved, waiver, qualifications, and AccessNI if the shift requires it.
  • Nobody got the urgent email: confirm SMTP in .env; away-mode volunteers are skipped on purpose.
  • Need a repeat shift fast: use Duplicate on Opportunities, or start from a template on Create opportunity.
