Accessibility statement

Last updated 23 November 2026.

This statement applies to ShelterLink at shelterlink.online, the volunteer rota used by Assisi Animal Sanctuary.

How accessible this site is

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA on volunteer-facing flows first, then staff screens. The site is partially compliant with that standard. Some parts do not yet meet every success criterion. Automated markup and contrast checks cover the redesigned public, volunteer, and staff surfaces; a full browser-based audit is not yet part of every release.

What we have in place

  • A skip link to the main content on each page, with a focusable main landmark.
  • One clear page title heading (h1) on public, volunteer, and staff pages.
  • Visible keyboard focus and respect for reduced motion preferences.
  • Form labels, error summaries, and named dialogs on the redesigned screens.
  • Status that is not colour alone (text labels with tinted badges).

Known limits

  • Some browser-native date pickers still use default form chrome.
  • A few admin filter and bulk-action forms still announce server errors in a live region rather than a focusable error summary.
  • Charts on the admin dashboard are not in scope for this pass.
  • Full axe or Lighthouse CI in a real browser is not wired into the test suite yet.

Reporting a problem

If you find a barrier, tell a member of staff at the sanctuary, or use the contact details on the Assisi website.

Say which page you were on, what you were trying to do, and which assistive technology you use if you know it. We will work with the sanctuary to improve the page where we can.

Enforcement

If you are not happy with how a report is handled, you can contact the Equality Commission for Northern Ireland or the Equality Advisory and Support Service. Details are on equalityni.org and equalityadvisoryservice.com.

Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on 23 November 2026 from the in-repo accessibility notes and automated checks in the ShelterLink test suite. It is not a claim of full WCAG certification.
