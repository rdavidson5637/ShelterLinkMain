How it works

Four steps from signing up to logging your first hours.

  1. Register Create an account with your name and contact details.
  2. Get approved Complete your profile. Staff review it before you can take shifts.
  3. Pick shifts Browse open shifts, apply, and wait for a confirmation.
  4. Log hours After each shift, record your hours and track your impact.
