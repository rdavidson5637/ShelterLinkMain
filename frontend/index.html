How a shift works

From first account to logged hours, without a spreadsheet or group chat.

  1. Register Create an account with your name and contact details.
  2. Get approved Finish your profile. Staff review it before you can take shifts.
  3. Pick a shift Browse open times, apply, and wait for confirmation when the shelter requires it.
  4. Log hours After the shift, record your hours so the day sheet stays accurate.
